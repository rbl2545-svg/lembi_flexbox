Reflection

The responsive card row was the hardest layout for me because I needed to understand how cards move onto new lines when the screen gets narrow. I looked up how flex-wrap and gap work together. I have seen navigation bars on shopping websites, where the logo is on one side and links are on the other. Centered hero sections appear on event websites to make the main message stand out. Card rows are common on restaurant menus, where each item has its own space. Building these examples helped me understand how Flexbox can arrange content in different ways. Testing the page at a smaller width also showed me why it is important to check a design on both phones and computers.
